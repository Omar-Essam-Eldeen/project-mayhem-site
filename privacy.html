Privacy Policy

PROJECT MAYHEM: SURVIVAL WEEK — LAST UPDATED 24 NOVEMBER 2026

Project Mayhem is a small, private cycle companion for two people. It is made for one couple and the people they choose to share it with. This page explains, in plain words, what the app collects and why.

What we collect

  • Your account: email address, nickname, date of birth, gender, phone number, and profile picture, if you add them.
  • Your cycle: period dates, cycle settings, daily logs, symptoms, and notes you write.
  • What you share: messages and photos you send to a synced partner, and the sharing choices you switch on.
  • Your device: a push-notification token, so the app can send you gentle reminders and discreet notes from your partner.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored on Supabase (servers in Frankfurt, Germany) and is used only to run the app: signing you in, syncing your data between your devices, and sending your reminders and partner notes. Emails (sign-up confirmation, password reset) are sent through Brevo. Push notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging.

What we never do

  • No advertising, no analytics, and no trackers.
  • We never sell your data, and we never share it with anyone except the services above that run the app.
  • Your cycle data is never put into a notification. Push notes only ever say something discreet, like “A new note arrived for you”.

Sharing with a partner

Sharing is off until you switch it on. You choose exactly which categories your partner can see (for example symptoms, mood, or your daily note), and you can stop sharing at any time. A partner never sees who else is synced with you — only how many accounts are synced. Partner syncing is controlled by the person whose cycle it is.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account inside the app (Settings — Delete account). That removes your profile and your data. You can also write to omar.essam.eldein@gmail.com and ask for your data to be deleted.

Age

Project Mayhem is for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here and the date above will change.
